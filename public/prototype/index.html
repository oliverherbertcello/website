About

Oliver Herbert is a concert cellist known for performances that combine intensity, clarity, and a strong sense of musical direction.

The recipient of a 2021 Avery Fisher Career Grant, his work spans solo performance, chamber music, and curatorial projects. Oliver approaches his projects with a wide-ranging curiosity, exploring lesser-known repertoire, music of today, and connections that reveal new perspectives on familiar works.
