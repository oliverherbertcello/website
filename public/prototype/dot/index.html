    About

    Oliver Herbert is a concert cellist known for performances that combine intensity, clarity, and a strong sense of musical direction.

    The recipient of a 2021 Avery Fisher Career Grant, his work spans solo performance, chamber music, and curatorial projects. Oliver approaches his projects with a wide-ranging curiosity, exploring lesser-known repertoire, music of today, and connections that reveal new perspectives on familiar works.

    Biography

    Oliver Herbert is a cellist who moves between concertos, chamber music and programs of his own making.

    The recipient of a 2021 Avery Fisher Career Grant and the Verbier Festival’s Jean-Nicolas Firmenich Prize, he has appeared with the San Francisco Symphony under Michael Tilson Thomas and with conductors including Giancarlo Guerrero, Juanjo Mena and Alexander Shelley. His chamber music has taken him to Marlboro, Carnegie Hall with Mitsuko Uchida, and festivals across the Americas and Europe.

    His projects often begin from a question rather than a repertoire list: what changes when we hear something twice, how power changes the people who hold it, where the music of the Americas and Europe meet. He plays a Guadagnini cello that once belonged to Antonio Janigro, on loan from the Janigro family.
